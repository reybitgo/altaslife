Ask us your question
What is AltasFarm Livelihood?

AltasFarm Livelihood is a community built around wellness and everyday products. Members serve repeat customers, support one another, and may advance through leadership ranks as they qualify.

Is registration really free?

Yes. Registering costs nothing, and there are no paid membership packages or entry tiers. Any product purchase is optional and separate from registration.

What products will be available?

The range may grow over time and may include wellness, herbal, organic and other everyday products. Current descriptions, prices and availability come from our team or the product catalog.

How do members take part in the livelihood opportunity?

Participation centers on qualifying repeat-purchase activity and building a team responsibly. The official program guide sets out the requirements, qualification rules and any compensation, so you can read them before you decide.

What does profit sharing mean?

Qualified ranks may be eligible to share in a designated portion of the business’s actual net profit. Eligibility and amounts follow the published policy, and no distribution is guaranteed.

Do I need sales or leadership experience?

No. You can inquire or register with no experience. Start by learning about the products and asking a team leader to explain the program and your options.

Will I earn just by inviting people to register?

Not on its own. Registering people is not an automatic entitlement to compensation — the program is built on real product activity, repeat customers and responsible team development.
